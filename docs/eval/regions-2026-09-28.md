# Where a search's hits live

2026-09-28. "A way in" (docs/PLAN.md): can a search say which region of
the library its results are in? `scripts/eval_regions.py` over the 62
queries of `tests/eval/queries-library.yaml` and the partition of
2026-09-27: 23 regions and 151 parts, 165 of them named and described.

## The right region

A query's expected documents are named by the set. Their entities belong
to regions and parts, and the one most of them belong to is the query's
right region. The right region is where the answers live. It is not a
judgement of what the question is about.

## Two ways of finding it

**Text:** the query's vector against each region's name, summary and
thirty heaviest members, embedded by the library's embedder.

**Hits:** the same count over the entities of the query's first five
search hits. The share the best region holds is the confidence.

| way | region right | part right |
|---|---|---|
| text | 0.45 | 0.44 |
| hits | 0.81 | 0.69 |

The text way is right under half the time, and its scores barely
separate right from wrong. It is not used.

The hits way, by the share the best region holds:

| shown when the share is at least | shown | right when shown |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 82% | 0.92 |
| 0.6 | 68% | 0.98 |
| 0.8 | 52% | 0.97 |

A part is chosen among its region's parts, so the page never names a
part of another region. By its share among them:

| shown when the share is at least | shown | right when shown |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 84% | 0.73 |
| 0.8 | 47% | 0.83 |
| 0.9 | 24% | 0.87 |

## Counted once, not weighed

The first version weighed each entity by how central it is to its region.
A live search for "Apfelkuchen" then showed the audio software region
(69%). Its first hits held an apple-cake recipe with seven entities in the
partition and two software notes with one each, and the software
entities' weight outvoted the recipe's seven. Counting each entity once,
for the right region and the shown one alike, is better on the set: the
region from 88% to 92% right at a share of 0.5, the part from 73% to 87%
at its threshold. The live searches after the change:

| query | weighed | counted |
|---|---|---|
| Apfelkuchen | audio synthesis › DAW software (0.69) | vegetarian recipes › international cuisine (0.75) |
| granular synthesis | audio synthesis (0.59) | time-frequency analysis (0.62) |
| counterpoint voice leading | music theory (0.73) | music theory (0.76) |
| wavelet transform synchrosqueezing | time-frequency analysis (1.00) | time-frequency analysis (0.99) |

Granular synthesis went the other way; why is not looked into here. One
query each way, and the set of 62 decided it.

## What it means

- `search` with `regions` opens with the region at half of the hits'
  entities, right 92% of the times it is shown, and with the part only at
  nine tenths (`store.REGION_SHARE`, `store.PART_SHARE`).
- The measurement leans towards the hits way. The right region comes from
  the expected documents, and the first hits often are those documents.
  That is also what the line claims: the region of your results. It is as
  good as the search is. A search that misses finds the region of what it
  found.
- Where it fails: 6 of the 34 keyword queries, 5 of the 18 paraphrases
  and 1 of the 10 structure queries name another region than the expected
  documents' own. Why each one does is not looked into here.
- A search that finds the wrong documents shows their region. The 2,902
  documents the NAS sent have no domain yet, so they are in every domain,
  and the word the graph adds for "Apfelkuchen" ("apple") finds Apple's
  QuickTime notes among them. That is the domains' question, not this
  one's.
- `ask` reading a region's summary: measured below, no gain.

## Ask, with the region's summary

`ask` can carry the region its passages come from, with the region's
summary and its parts, as the library's map for the model to describe
and not to cite (`regions`). `scripts/eval_ask_regions.py` asked one
question per named region, "what does my library hold on <its name>?",
without the region and with it, with no surfing steps and the host's
local model. Breadth is the share of the region's twenty heaviest members
the answer names.

| | without the region | with it |
|---|---|---|
| breadth | 0.24 | 0.25 |
| passages cited | 7.5 | 7.6 |
| words | 229 | 236 |

Of the 21 regions, 8 answers were broader with it, 5 narrower, 8 the
same. The region in the bundle was the one asked about for 16. The model
writes from its passages, and a summary beside them adds nothing a count
can see. The flag stays off. It stays in the code as a choice, and the
plan item closes on this measurement.

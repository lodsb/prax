# Where a search's hits live

2026-09-28. "A way in" (docs/PLAN.md): can a search say which region of
the library its results are in? `scripts/eval_regions.py` over the 62
queries of `tests/eval/queries-library.yaml` and the partition of
2026-09-27: 23 regions and 151 parts, 165 of them named and described.

## The right region

A query's expected documents are named by the set. Their entities belong
to regions and parts, and the one holding most of their weight is the
query's right region. An entity's weight is how central it is to its
region. The right region is where the answers live. It is not a
judgement of what the question is about.

## Two ways of finding it

**Text:** the query's vector against each region's name, summary and
thirty heaviest members, embedded by the library's embedder.

**Hits:** the same weighing as for the right region, over the entities of
the query's first five search hits. The share of the weight the best
region holds is the confidence.

| way | region right | part right |
|---|---|---|
| text | 0.50 | 0.44 |
| hits | 0.82 | 0.61 |

The text way is right half the time at best. Its scores barely separate:
at 0.75 it shows a region for 11% of queries and is right for 71% of
those. It is not used.

The hits way, by the share the best region holds:

| shown when the share is at least | shown | right when shown |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 81% | 0.88 |
| 0.6 | 68% | 0.90 |
| 0.7 | 53% | 0.97 |
| 0.9 | 32% | 1.00 |

A part is chosen among its region's parts, so the page never names a
part of another region. By its share among them:

| shown when the share is at least | shown | right when shown |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 79% | 0.73 |
| 0.7 | 55% | 0.76 |
| 0.9 | 26% | 0.88 |

## What it means

- `search` with `regions` opens with the region at half the weight,
  right 88% of the times it is shown, and with the part only at nine
  tenths (`store.REGION_SHARE`, `store.PART_SHARE`).
- The measurement leans towards the hits way. The right region comes from
  the expected documents, and the first hits often are those documents.
  That is also what the line claims: the region of your results. It is as
  good as the search is. A search that misses finds the region of what it
  found.
- Where it fails: 5 of the 34 keyword queries, 5 of the 18 paraphrases
  and 1 of the 10 structure queries name another region than the expected
  documents' own. Why each one does is not looked into here.
- `ask` reading a region's summary for a question about a region is not
  measured yet. It wants its own set of questions about regions, and the
  judge of `eval_ask.py`.

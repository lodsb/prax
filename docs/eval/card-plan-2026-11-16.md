# The card plan, measured (stage AI), 2026-11-16

What the plan of 2026-10-02 (`prax.host.plan`, `prax up` following it)
did to how long readings wait, and how many swaps it made. Taken with
`scripts/eval_swaps.py --days 45` on the live store, read-only, from
`run/swaps.jsonl` (written since 2026-10-05) and the `readings` queue
(since 2026-09-23).

## Swaps

15 swaps in six weeks: 12 by the plan, 3 asked for by a person, and 14
give-backs. At most 4 in one day (2026-11-03, during marker's run of 530
readings). 9 of the 15 were made with `fits: false`, meaning marker took
the card while llama-server held it, so llama-server was stopped for it.

There is no count from before the plan: the record started three days
after it. The rule it replaced (`swap: auto`, a swap as soon as
anything waits) would have swapped on every reading that waited for
the other role.

## Waits

A reading's wait is from `at` (asked) to `finished_at`. The requests
are split three ways. The machine's are those of the clock, the door
and the OCR window. A person's request is small when at most 10
readings were asked in one minute; more is a backlog handed over.

Before the plan (2026-09-23 to 10-01), two small requests for marker
waited 39.4 h each, and two for vision-pages 15.1 h. These are the
case of 2026-10-01 that started the stage: marker was `on_demand` and
waited for a person to start it.

## Reading

- What the plan promises is that a person's small request goes next.
  It does: 17 marker requests waited a median of 34 minutes, at most
  3 h, against 39 h before. The samples are small: 2 before, 17 after.
- Backlogs wait hours to a day, which is what the plan lets them do:
  a backlog waits for the night or until it is large.
- The waits past 100 h are one event, not the plan's. 185 readings were
  asked between 2026-10-17 and 10-29, while the card could not run
  (the power supply, 2026-10-17 to 11-02), and all finished on 11-02,
  when the bigger supply was in. Before that, 50 figure readings asked
  by the clock on 10-12 waited until 10-17.
- The plan does not see work outside prax. On 2026-11-16 two training
  runs of another project held the card at 99% with 4.5 GB. The fit
  check counts memory (`nvidia-smi` totals), so a model that does not
  fit beside them is not loaded. Compute shared with them is not
  counted; a reading on a shared card is slower, and its wait shows it.
